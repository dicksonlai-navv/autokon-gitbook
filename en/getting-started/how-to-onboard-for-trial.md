# How To Onboard For Trial

{% hint style="info" %}
Please [book a discovery call](https://tidycal.com/dickson/unit-handover-inspection-demo-with-autokon) with us to discuss about your interest to do a trial with us.
{% endhint %}

## Step 1: Project Setup

We configure your project data and onboard all users with login access.

* Trial scope (units, timeline, roles) confirmed with all parties.
* Create WhatsApp group with PICs for coordination and support.
* [Project information](https://form.jotform.com/241931776009460) are pre-loaded into the AutoKon system.
* Developer PICs and Contractor PICs to receive login credentials.
* Contact details of Tara (our AI WhatsApp) will be shared for defect management.

## Step 2: Online Workshop

A live walkthrough to train your team on how to use AutoKon effectively.

* A 1-hour session is conducted via Google Meets
* Live walkthrough of key features:
  * Inspection Scheduling
  * WhatsApp-based Defect Logging
  * Defect Rectification Workflow Management
* Open Q\&A to ensure everyone is confident before the trial starts

## Step 3: Trial Begins

Real unit inspections, defect logging, and rectification tracking are carried out using AutoKon.

* Real unit inspections are scheduled using AutoKon
* Defects are logged on-site via WhatsApp by Inspectors
* Contractors acknowledge defect assignments and set expected rectification timeline
* Contractors upload rectification updates via WhatsApp
* AutoKon serves as the central platform for all inspection schedules and defect rectification activities

## Step 4: Monitoring & Support

We provide real-time support and monitors platform usage to ensure a smooth experience.

* We offer daily support through WhatsApp group and onsite (if required).
* We track system usage and responds to issues in real time.

## Last Step: Trial Review & Next Steps

We review trial results together and align on next steps for rollout or enhancements.

* Trial data is compiled: number of inspections, defects, rectifications, user feedback
* A debrief session is held to present results and gather final input
* Discussion on rollout options, system enhancements, or integration needs
