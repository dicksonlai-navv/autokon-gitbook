# How To Acknowledge Assigned Defects

## Step 1: Go To Defect Management

Login to AutoKon WebApp and navigate to the **Defect Management > Overview** menu from the sidebar.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/app.autokon.id_contractor_defects (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 2: **Select Unit & View Details**

Choose the unit and click **“View Details”** to see defect information.

## Step 3: **Select Defects to Acknowledge**

Select one or more defects to acknowledge.

{% hint style="info" %}
&#x20;Tip: Apply the filter to display only defects with the status “Pending Acknowledge”.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/app.autokon.id_contractor_defects (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 4: **Set Defect Rectification Timeline**

Select the **expected start and end dates** for rectification of the selected defects.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/app.autokon.id_contractor_defects (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Last Step: Confirm Acknowledgement

Review the details and click **“Confirm”** to complete the process.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/app.autokon.id_contractor_defects (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

✅ The defect status will update automatically to “Pending Rectification”, and both your real estate developer and customer will be notified.
